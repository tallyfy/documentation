## What are Tallyfy's how-to guides?

These guides show you how to solve common workflow challenges using Tallyfy. Each one covers a specific business situation and walks you through an effective approach.

They're a complement to the feature documentation, showing how you can combine Tallyfy's features to tackle real-world problems.

### Why use these guides?

- **Task-focused instructions** - step-by-step help for common workflow issues
- **Implementation approaches** - ideas based on real Tallyfy customer implementations
- **Efficiency tips** - suggestions to help your team work more productively
- **Process improvement** - ideas for making your business workflows more effective
