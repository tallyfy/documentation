## How can I make process improvements stick?

Embed your changes directly into Tallyfy workflows - don't rely on training alone. Rolling a change out and keeping it in place are the "Improve" and "Control" phases of [DMAIC](/products/pro/tutorials/how-to/process-improvement/introduction-to-dmaic-for-process-improvement/).

### Implementing changes effectively

Traditional training fails because of the [Ebbinghaus forgetting curve](/products/pro/tutorials/why-documentation-training-fails-forgetting-curve/) - people forget what they've learned unless something reinforces it. Instead of relying on memory, build the new process right into the workflow.

**How to do it in Tallyfy:** Modify your [procedure templates](/products/pro/documenting/templates/) or [document templates](/products/pro/documenting/documents/). Every new [process](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/processes/) launched from that template automatically uses the updated version. Processes already running keep the version they launched with. There's no paper redistribution and no outdated copies floating around.

### Sustaining improvements over time

*   **Standardization** - When people run processes inside Tallyfy, they work through the steps of the updated template, so they follow the new standard by default instead of from memory.
*   **Visibility and monitoring** - Use [Tallyfy Analytics](/products/pro/integrations/analytics/) and the [Tracker view](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/tracker-view/) to check whether people are following the new process and it's delivering results. The Tracker shows which processes are overdue or have unresolved problems. Analytics is an add-on that records when each task is completed, so you can compare completion times before and after the change.
*   **Feedback loops** - Use [Tallyfy's comment features](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/tasks/how-can-i-manage-comments-in-tallyfy/) to gather ongoing feedback. This lets your team flag issues and suggest tweaks without waiting for the next scheduled review.
*   **Regular reviews** - Schedule periodic reviews of key processes. At each one, check the Tracker's **Overdue processes** and **Overdue problems** presets and compare completion times with earlier months to spot new bottlenecks.
