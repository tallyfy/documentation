## How to change a member into a guest

Deactivate the member's account first, then convert them to a guest. It's a two-step process.

This helps when someone was invited as a [member](/products/pro/documenting/members/) by mistake and should have been a [guest](/products/pro/documenting/guests/), or when a teammate moves outside your company but still needs to complete the occasional task.

### What you need
- Administrator permissions
- The person must be a [member](/products/pro/documenting/members/) of your Tallyfy organization

### Step 1: Deactivate the member account

You can't convert an active member directly - deactivate their account first.
<Steps>
1. Go to **Settings** > **Organization** > **Members**.
2. Click the name of the member you want to change.
3. Scroll down and click **Deactivate Account**.
   ![Deactivate account option in Tallyfy member settings](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy%2Fpro%2Fdesktop-light-remove-member1.png)
4. Tallyfy asks what to do with their incomplete [tasks](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/tasks/). Two options:
   - **Reassign tasks** - transfer their work to other team members
   - **Deactivate without reassigning** - keep tasks where they are (you can reassign later)
</Steps>

### Step 2: Change the deactivated account to guest

<Steps>
1. Return to **Settings** > **Organization** > **Members**.
2. Find the deactivated person. If you don't see them, set the status filter to **Deactivated**.
3. Click **Convert to guest** next to their name.
</Steps>

That's it.

### What happens after conversion

The change takes effect immediately:

- Their account switches from [member](/products/pro/documenting/members/) to [guest](/products/pro/documenting/guests/) status.
- They lose dashboard access - no more logging into Tallyfy directly.
- They can *only* work on [tasks](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/tasks/) through [guest links](/products/pro/documenting/guests/how-to-find-a-guest-task-link-in-tallyfy/) sent by email.
- They're removed from any groups and lose their member permissions.
- You free up a paid member seat.

### When conversion is blocked

Tallyfy rejects the conversion and changes nothing in these cases:

- **The member is still active.** Deactivate them first (Step 1), then convert.
- **The only administrator** can't be deactivated, so they can't be converted. Your organization always needs at least one admin.
- **Trial plans at the guest limit** can't add the new guest. Remove an unused guest or upgrade first.
- **Your free trial has ended** without a paid plan. The account is read-only until you upgrade, so no member changes go through. Contact support if you need more time.
