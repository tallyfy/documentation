Use Tallyfy [automations](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/) to route [tasks](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/tasks/) to different team members based on customer service levels. An automation reads the customer's tier from a kick-off form field and assigns the person or group you picked for that tier, so nobody has to route each one by hand.

## When you'd use service-based routing

Use it wherever the customer's tier decides who does the work. A support team might give enterprise customers a dedicated team and send free users to standard support. Sales teams hand high-value deals to senior closers. In professional services, complex projects go to senior consultants and simple ones to junior staff. Subscription onboarding works the same way, with your best people on enterprise accounts and free trials left to whoever is available.

## Setting up service-based assignment

### Basic service level routing

**Scenario**: You're onboarding clients who each buy one of three service packages, and Platinum customers need your best account manager.

1. **Add a service selection field to your [kick-off form](/products/pro/launching/triggers/kick-off-forms/)[^1]**:
   - Field type: Dropdown or Radio
   - Options: "Basic", "Professional", "Platinum"
   - Label: "Service Package"

2. **Create an assignment automation for each level**:

   **Automation 1: Platinum service**
   ```automation
   AUTOMATION Platinum service
   IF field "Service Package" is "Platinum"
   THEN replace assignees on "Prepare proposal" with "Sarah Chen"
   AND replace assignees on "Account setup" with "Sarah Chen"
   ```

   **Automation 2: Professional service**
   ```automation
   AUTOMATION Professional service
   IF field "Service Package" is "Professional"
   THEN replace assignees on "Prepare proposal" with "Mike Johnson"
   AND replace assignees on "Account setup" with "Mike Johnson"
   ```

   **Automation 3: Basic service**
   ```automation
   AUTOMATION Basic service
   IF field "Service Package" is "Basic"
   THEN replace assignees on "Prepare proposal" with group "Support Team"
   AND replace assignees on "Account setup" with group "Support Team"
   ```

### Advanced patterns

#### Sharing the work with a group

Don't pile everything on one person. Assign to a group instead, and name each step the
automation should cover:

```automation
AUTOMATION Platinum work goes to the senior group
IF field "Service Package" is "Platinum"
THEN replace assignees on "Prepare proposal" with group "Senior Account Managers"
AND replace assignees on "Account setup" with group "Senior Account Managers"
```

When you assign a group, every member of it becomes an assignee. One of them completing the task
is enough, unless the step's **All assignees must complete this task** setting is turned on. Each
action names one step, so add an action for every step you want the group to pick up.

#### Multi-criteria assignment

Combine multiple conditions when service level alone isn't enough:

```automation
IF field "Service Package" is "Platinum"
AND field "Industry" is "Healthcare"
THEN replace assignees on "Compliance review" with group "Healthcare Specialist Team"
```

Both conditions must be true, so only Platinum customers in healthcare get the specialist team. It uses the same [replace assignees](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/actions/asignment-actions/) action as the automations above.

#### Extra review steps for higher tiers

Use [visibility actions](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/actions/visibility-actions/) to show an approval step only to the tiers that need it:

```automation
IF field "Service Package" is "Basic"
THEN hide "Executive review"

IF field "Service Package" is "Platinum"
THEN show "Executive review"
AND add assignees to "Executive review" with group "C-Suite"
```

## Example: technical support tiers

**Kick-off form fields**:
- Customer tier (dropdown): "Free", "Starter", "Growth", "Enterprise"
- Issue type (dropdown): "Bug", "Feature Request", "Integration Help"

**The automations**:

Give **Initial response** a default deadline of 48 hours after launch in its [step timings](/products/pro/documenting/templates/edit-templates/step-timings/). A deadline action can't count from the launch itself, so the faster tiers count back from that step's own deadline.

1. **Free tier, community support**:
   ```automation
   AUTOMATION Free tier support
   IF field "Customer Tier" is "Free"
   THEN add assignees to "Initial response" with group "Community Managers"
   ```

2. **Starter tier, standard support**:
   ```automation
   AUTOMATION Starter tier support
   IF field "Customer Tier" is "Starter"
   THEN add assignees to "Initial response" with group "Tier 1 Support"
   AND shift deadline of "Initial response" by 24 hours before its own deadline
   ```

3. **Growth tier, priority support**:
   ```automation
   AUTOMATION Growth tier support
   IF field "Customer Tier" is "Growth"
   THEN add assignees to "Initial response" with group "Tier 2 Support"
   AND shift deadline of "Initial response" by 44 hours before its own deadline
   ```

4. **Enterprise, dedicated support**:
   ```automation
   AUTOMATION Enterprise tier support
   IF field "Customer Tier" is "Enterprise"
   THEN add assignees to "Initial response" with group "Enterprise Success Team"
   AND shift deadline of "Initial response" by 47 hours before its own deadline
   AND show "Executive escalation"
   ```

Free keeps the 48-hour default, so Starter's first response is due 24 hours after launch, Growth's 4 hours and Enterprise's 1 hour. These automations read only kick-off form fields, so Tallyfy [runs them once, at launch](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/when-automations-run/). Changing the customer tier after launch doesn't reassign the task, so reassign it by hand if the wrong tier was picked.

## Best practices

- **Assign groups, not individuals.** Every group member is an assignee, so the task doesn't stall while one person is on vacation.
- **Cover enterprise time zones.** Enterprise customers in other regions shouldn't wait for your local team to start work, so give them a group with people in their time zone.
- **Check workload monthly.** Filter the [Tasks view](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/tasks-view/) by assignee to spot uneven workload before it causes burnout.
- **Write down what each tier includes**, such as its response time, and tell customers before they buy, so your team and your customers expect the same thing.
