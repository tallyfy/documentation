## Automation examples

Each example below is a tested Tallyfy automation pattern. Its page shows the conditions, the actions and how to set them up in your template.

import { AutomationTypes } from "~/components";

<AutomationTypes />

### Assignment

**[Assign to process requestor](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/examples/assign-step-to-requestor/)**
Routes a later step back to whoever launched the process. An assignee picker field on the kick-off form records that person, so nobody has to remember who started the request.

**[Dynamic member assignment from kick-off forms](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/examples/dynamic-member-assignment-from-kickoff/)**
The person launching the process picks who handles each task in assignee picker fields on the kick-off form, and automations assign them. Use it when different projects need different people.

**[Service level-based task assignment](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/examples/service-level-assignment/)**
Routes tasks by the service tier picked on the kick-off form, such as Basic, Professional or Platinum, so each tier's tasks go to its own person or group.

**[Notify the requestor when the process completes](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/examples/notify-requestor-on-completion/)**
An assignee picker on the kick-off form records who asked, and an automation assigns an Email Auto-Send step to that person, which emails them the outcome.

### Workflow control

**[Exclusive choice (pick one path)](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/examples/exclusive-choice/)**
The process follows one branch, never both. Office workers get IT access and a desk; field workers get safety training and equipment setup. A radio button field holds one role, so only one automation fires.

**[Synchronization (wait for multiple approvals)](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/examples/synchronization/)**
Conditions joined with AND keep the next steps hidden until every required approval comes back approved.

**[Multi-level approval loops](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/examples/multi-level-approval-loops/)**
A rejection reopens the request for fixes. This works once at each approval level in a process, so after a second rejection at the same level, reopen the request by hand.

**[Decision with waiting step](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/examples/decision-wait-continue/)**
A YES answer goes straight to the next step and a NO answer shows a waiting step first. A third automation shows the next step once the waiting step is completed.

### Time-based

**[Countdown deadline automation](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/examples/countdown-deadline-automation/)**
Makes a task due a set time before a date someone enters, such as an agenda due 1 day before a meeting.

**[Combined visibility and deadline automations](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/examples/combined-visibility-deadline-rules/)**
One automation shows an international shipping step only for international shipments. A second one sets its deadline from the expected arrival date.

### Quick reference

- **Customer support routing** → [Service level-based assignment](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/examples/service-level-assignment/)
- **Purchase approvals** → [Multi-level approval loops](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/examples/multi-level-approval-loops/)
- **Event planning** → [Countdown deadline automation](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/examples/countdown-deadline-automation/)
- **Project delegation** → [Dynamic member assignment](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/examples/dynamic-member-assignment-from-kickoff/)
- **Compliance workflows** → [Synchronization](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/examples/synchronization/)

For BPMN-based process patterns, see [BPMN Examples and Patterns](https://tallyfy.com/bpmn-examples-and-patterns).
