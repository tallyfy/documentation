## Decision with waiting step

This pattern needs three [automations](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/). It handles "Is something ready?" - either proceed immediately or wait until it is.

### The pattern

- **YES** - skip waiting, go directly to the next step
- **NO** - show a waiting step, then continue when it's done

One automation for YES and one for NO only handle the decision. A third automation shows the next step once the waiting step is completed.

### Example: payment confirmation

Say you have three steps:
1. **Decision step** - "Has the customer paid?" (YES/NO [field](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/tasks/what-are-form-fields-in-tallyfy/))
2. **Waiting step** - "Hold until payment received" (starts hidden, because an automation shows it)
3. **Confirmation step** - "Record payment evidence" (starts hidden, because an automation shows it)

### All three automations

Each one is a [visibility action](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/actions/visibility-actions/) that shows or hides a step.

**Automation 1 - YES path** (customer already paid):
```automation
IF field "Payment" is "Yes"
THEN hide "Hold until payment received"
AND show "Record payment evidence"
```

A customer who has already paid skips the wait and goes straight to confirmation.

**Automation 2 - NO path** (customer hasn't paid yet):
```automation
IF field "Payment" is "No"
THEN show "Hold until payment received"
AND hide "Record payment evidence"
```

For an unpaid customer, the waiting step appears and confirmation stays hidden until automation 3 shows it.

**Automation 3 - Waiting complete** (payment has now arrived):
```automation
IF step "Hold until payment received" is completed
THEN show "Record payment evidence"
```

When someone completes the waiting step, this automation shows the confirmation step.

:::warning[Common mistake]
Without automation 3, the confirmation step stays hidden after the waiting step is completed, unless someone [shows it by hand](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/tasks/manually-show-hide-tasks/). The NO path dead-ends.
:::

### Why you need all three

Here's what happens with only two automations:

| Scenario | What happens |
|----------|--------------|
| YES selected | Works fine - confirmation step appears |
| NO selected | Waiting step appears, confirmation hidden |
| Waiting step completed | No automation reads the waiting step, so confirmation stays hidden |
