## How do I email someone outside my organization who has no task?

Use an Email step, not a Task. Collect the person's email address in a short text field, then pick that field in the Email step's **To** row. They get one email from your process, with no task to open and no reminders.

For example, a property manager can email owners, tenants and tradespeople at the right point in each process. None of them needs to sign in to Tallyfy.

### Why not a task?

Assign a Task to an outside email address and that person becomes a guest with a task to do. They get an email saying they've been assigned, with a link to open the task, and any reminder your team sends goes to them too. That's right when they need to do something in Tallyfy. It's confusing when you only wanted to tell them something. [Guests](/products/pro/documenting/guests/#what-does-a-guest-get-on-a-task-step-and-an-email-step) compares the two.

### Prerequisites

- Admin or Standard member role to edit templates.
- A plan that runs [automations](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/), such as Pro or Enterprise, or an active trial. Picking a field in **To** saves an automation.

### Setting it up

<Steps>
1. **Collect the email address**

   On the [kick-off form](/products/pro/launching/triggers/kick-off-forms/), add a **Short Text Field**, such as "Owner email", and make it required. It needs the email check, **Enforce a single email address only**, which is turned on for you when you pick the field in step 3.

2. **Add the Email step**

   Add a step, open its **Edit step** panel, and pick **Email** in the **Type** row. Under **When it goes out**, pick **Sends itself on the deadline** or **Someone checks it and presses Send**. The first sends the email at the step's deadline, and a member assigned to the step can click **Send Right Now** to send it sooner. With the second, someone on your team can edit the email, then clicks **Send**. A guest can't send it.

   The step's title is the email's subject, and you can insert fields in it as [variables](/products/pro/documenting/templates/variables/how-can-i-use-insert-variables-in-tallyfy/). A title of "Your lease renewal for" plus the property address field gives each owner their own address in the subject.

   Write the message itself in the step's description. If the description is empty, nothing is sent.

   Leave **Send as a plain email** on. It's on by default for a new Email step, and the email arrives as a plain message. Turn it off and it arrives inside a styled email with your logo at the top.

   Images, tables and headings in the description reach the person only when **Send as a plain email** is on. With it off, they're removed from the email. With it on, an image keeps its size but not its alignment, so it sits on the left. Resize the image file itself to the width you want.

3. **Make the field the recipient**

   In the Email step's **To** row, choose the **Fields** filter and pick your email field. A line under the picker says when the person is added, such as "Whoever is entered in Owner email is added when the process starts." This is saved as an automation that adds whoever is entered to **To**. [Assign a step from an earlier answer](/products/pro/documenting/templates/edit-templates/assign-steps-in-the-tallyfy-template-builder/#from-an-earlier-answer) has the details.

   The field sits beside anyone else in **To**, and they all get the email. For **Sends itself on the deadline**, leave nobody else there if only the outside person should get it. Take **Whoever launches** out of **To** too. A launch from the launch screen still puts the person launching on a step with no member or group in **To**, so they'd get a copy. To send to the outside person alone, pick the field with the **Fields** filter first. Then open the automation it saved, "Assign" plus the step and field names, on the template's **Automations** screen, and change **Add assignees** to **Replace assignees**. It keeps the field, and **Replace assignees** takes the person launching off the step. After the change, the field no longer shows in **To**, so **To** looks empty and the empty-field warning goes too. Anyone you add to **To** later is taken off again when the automation runs, so change the recipients in that automation, not in **To**.

   For **Someone checks it and presses Send**, someone on your team has to press **Send**. So keep the team member who'll check the email in **To** as well. They get a copy when it goes out, which is expected.

4. **Set the deadline**

   For **Sends itself on the deadline**, the deadline is when the email goes out. Set it for after the address will be filled in. If nobody is in **To** when the deadline arrives, the step completes and nothing is sent.
</Steps>

Before going live, launch a test process with an address of yours from outside your organization in the field, such as a personal one, so the email comes to you. Each address you test with becomes a guest of your organization.

### Using a field on an earlier step

The address doesn't have to come from the kick-off form. The **Fields** filter also lists short text fields on the steps before this one, such as a tradesperson's address entered on a "Book the repair" step. The person is added when that step is completed, so:

- Make the field required, so nobody can complete the step without an address.
- Set the Email step's deadline to count from the step that holds the field. While that step is still open, an Email that sends itself doesn't send, even once its deadline has passed.
- Don't send the Email step by hand before that step is completed. If someone clicks **Send Right Now** or **Send** early, the email goes only to the people already in **To**, the step completes, and the outside person is never added.

The automation behind the field runs once in each process. If someone fixes a mistyped address after it has run, the email still goes to the old one, so [change the step's assignees](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/tasks/edit-task/how-can-i-assign-members-to-tasks-in-tallyfy/) in the running process instead.

### What the person receives

They get one email when the step sends:

- The subject is the step's title, with any inserted fields filled in.
- The message is the step's description.
- With **Send as a plain email** on, that's all they see. With it off, the email also shows your logo at the top and a **Manage Preferences** link.

With **Send as a plain email** on, the summary under the step's switches says so: "The people in To get this email and nothing else: no task and no reminders." Plain or not, for that step they don't get:

- An email saying they've been assigned.
- Reminders, including one your team sends by hand.
- Emails about comments, unless a comment mentions them or they watch the step.
- A button to open a task, or a line about form fields.

Their guest digest doesn't list the step either.

If the person has turned off task emails for your organization, or chosen **Opt out of all emails**, the step's email isn't sent to them. The team member who sent it, or the process owner, is usually told by email instead.

### Emailing several kinds of people

Give each kind of person their own email field. A property manager might have "Owner email", "Tenant email" and "Tradesperson email" fields.

- To send each of them a different email, add one Email step for each kind and pick that kind's field in its **To**.
- To send them all the same email, pick all three fields in one Email step's **To**. A launch from the launch screen also sends the person launching a copy. Changing these automations to **Replace assignees** doesn't help here, because each one would replace the others.

### How do I send an outside person an update when part of the process is done?

Add an Email that sends itself after each milestone, and count its deadline from that milestone's last step. While that step is still open, the email waits. Once it's completed, the email goes out at the deadline.

1. Add an Email step right after the milestone's last step. Under **When it goes out**, pick **Sends itself on the deadline**, and write the update as its title and description.
2. In its **Edit step** panel, open the **Finish** row. Under **Counts from**, pick the milestone's last step, and set a short gap, such as 1 hour.
3. In its **To** row, choose the **Fields** filter and pick the same email field. If only the outside person should get the update, change the automation this saves to **Replace assignees**, as step 3 of the setup explains.

Repeat this for each milestone you want to report on.

Watching the process isn't the way to do this for an outside person. A guest can only watch what they're assigned to, and the emails a watcher gets link into Tallyfy.

### Troubleshooting

**Nothing was sent?**
- Check the email field had an address in it. If it's the only recipient, the editor warns "If Owner email is empty, nobody gets this email." After a change to **Replace assignees**, that warning no longer shows, so make the field required.
- Check the step's description isn't empty.
- Check your plan runs automations. Pro, Enterprise and AppSumo plans and active trials do. On Basic and Docs plans, and a free plan whose trial has ended, the **Fields** filter is greyed out and says **Automations do not run on this plan**.
- Each address becomes a guest in your organization, and a trial has a guest limit. At that limit, a new address can't be added.
- Check whether the person turned off task emails or chose **Opt out of all emails**. If they did, someone on your team was usually told instead.
- An Email that sends itself waits for its deadline, so check the deadline has come.
- If nobody was in **To** at the deadline, the step completed without sending. Set the deadline later, or move the field earlier.

**The person got a task instead of an email?**
- The step is a Task. Change its **Type** to **Email**.

**Someone on your team got a copy?**
- With **Someone checks it and presses Send**, that's expected: the person who sends it is in **To**.
- With **Sends itself on the deadline**, they're in **To** by mistake. Take them out. If it's the person who launched the process, change the field's automation to **Replace assignees**, as step 3 explains.
