## How do I email someone outside my organization who has no task?

Use an Email step, not a Task. Collect the person's email address in a form field, then pick that field in the Email step's **To** row. They get one email from your process, with no task to open and no reminders.

For example, a property manager can email owners, tenants and tradespeople at the right point in each process. None of them needs to sign in to Tallyfy.

### Why not a Task?

Assign a Task to an outside email address and that person becomes a guest with a task to do. Tallyfy emails them to say they've been assigned, with a link to open the task, and any reminder your team sends goes to them too. That's right when they need to do something in Tallyfy. It's confusing when you only wanted to tell them something. [Guests](/products/pro/documenting/guests/#what-does-a-guest-get-on-a-task-step-and-an-email-step) compares the two.

### Prerequisites

- Admin or Standard member role to edit templates
- A Pro plan (or active trial). Picking a field in **To** saves an [automation](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/), and automations don't run on other plans

### Setting it up

<Steps>
1. **Collect the email address**

   On the [kick-off form](/products/pro/launching/triggers/kick-off-forms/), add a **Short Text Field**, such as "Owner email", and make it required. It needs the email check, **Enforce a single email address only**, and Tallyfy turns that on for you when you pick the field in step 3.

2. **Add the Email step**

   Add a step, open its **Edit step** panel, and pick **Email** in the **Type** row. Under **When it goes out**, pick one:
   - **Sends itself on the deadline**: the email goes out at the step's deadline. A member assigned to the step can click **Send Right Now** to send it sooner.
   - **Someone checks it and presses Send**: someone on your team can edit the email, then clicks **Send**. A guest can't send it.

   The step's title is the email's subject, and you can insert fields in it as [variables](/products/pro/documenting/templates/variables/how-can-i-use-insert-variables-in-tallyfy/). A title of "Your lease renewal for" plus the property address field gives each owner their own address in the subject.

   Write the message itself in the step's description. If the description is empty, nothing is sent.

   Leave **Send as a plain email** on. It's on by default for a new Email step, and the email arrives as a plain message. Turn it off and it arrives inside a styled email with your logo at the top.

   Images reach the person only when **Send as a plain email** is on. With it off, Tallyfy removes them from the email. With it on, an image keeps its size but not its alignment, so it sits on the left. Resize the image file itself to the width you want.

3. **Make the field the recipient**

   In the Email step's **To** row, choose the **Fields** filter and pick your email field. A line under the picker says when the person is added, such as "Whoever is entered in Owner email is added when the process starts." Tallyfy saves this as an automation that adds whoever is entered to **To**. [Assign a step from an earlier answer](/products/pro/documenting/templates/edit-templates/assign-steps-in-the-tallyfy-template-builder/#from-an-earlier-answer) has the details.

   The field sits beside anyone else in **To**, and they all get the email. So leave nobody else there if only the outside person should get it. A step with nobody else in **To** also goes to the person who launches the process, unless you turn off **Assign launcher automatically?** for that step.

   With **Someone checks it and presses Send**, someone on your team has to press **Send**. So add the team member who'll check the email to **To** as well. They get a copy when it goes out.

4. **Set the deadline**

   For **Sends itself on the deadline**, the deadline is when the email goes out. Set it for after the address will be filled in. If nobody is in **To** when the deadline arrives, the step completes and nothing is sent.
</Steps>

Before going live, launch a test process with your own email address in the field, so the email comes to you.

### Using a field on an earlier step

The address doesn't have to come from the kick-off form. The **Fields** filter also lists short text fields on the steps before this one, such as a tradesperson's address entered on a "Book the repair" step. The person is added when that step is completed, so:

- Make the field required, so nobody can complete the step without an address.
- Set the Email step's deadline to count from the step that holds the field. While that step is still open, an Email that sends itself waits, so the email can't go out before the address is there.

### What the person receives

They get one email when the step sends:

- The subject is the step's title, with any inserted fields filled in.
- The message is the step's description.
- With **Send as a plain email** on, that's all they see. With it off, the email also shows your logo at the top and a **Manage Preferences** link.

The summary under the step's switches says the same: "The people in To get this email and nothing else: no task and no reminders." For that step, Tallyfy doesn't send them:

- An email saying they've been assigned.
- Reminders, including one your team sends by hand.
- Emails about comments, unless a comment mentions them or they watch the step.
- A button to open a task, or a line about form fields.

Their guest digest doesn't list the step either.

### Emailing several kinds of people

Give each kind of person their own email field. A property manager might have "Owner email", "Tenant email" and "Tradesperson email" fields.

- To send each of them a different email, add one Email step for each kind and pick that kind's field in its **To**.
- To send them all the same email, pick all three fields in one Email step's **To**.

### Troubleshooting

**Nothing was sent?**
- Check the email field had an address in it. If it's the only recipient, the editor warns "If Owner email is empty, nobody gets this email."
- Check the step's description isn't empty.
- Check your organization is on a Pro plan or an active trial. On other plans the **Fields** filter is greyed out and says **Automations do not run on this plan**.
- An Email that sends itself waits for its deadline, so check the deadline has come.
- If nobody was in **To** at the deadline, the step completed without sending. Set the deadline later, or move the field earlier.

**The person got a task instead of an email?**
- The step is a Task. Change its **Type** to **Email**.

**Someone on your team got a copy?**
- They're in the step's **To**. Take them out, or turn off **Assign launcher automatically?** for that step.
