Tallyfy lets you set [task](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/tasks/) deadlines that count backwards from a target date. Your meeting agenda can be automatically due 1 day before the meeting. That's how preparation tasks always finish before the critical date arrives.

:::note[Countdowns follow a date that moves]
A countdown deadline follows its target date. If the meeting is rescheduled, change the date and
complete the form or step that holds it again. Every countdown deadline counting from that date
moves to match. A deadline someone changed by hand on a task stays where they put it. See [when
an automation runs](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/when-automations-run/) for
the detail.
:::

## When to use countdown deadlines

- **Meeting prep** - reminders 1 day before scheduled meetings
- **Event planning** - setup tasks due 2 weeks before the event
- **Launch coordination** - marketing materials due 1 week before launch
- **Contract renewals** - start discussions 30 days before expiration
- **Compliance** - submit reports 5 days before regulatory due dates

You're working backwards from a fixed date. Everything lands on time.

Each task has no dependency on the others. They are four independent offsets from one date field, which is why they all point at the same anchor.

## How to create countdown deadline automations

<Steps>
1. **Add a date field for the target date**

   In your kick-off form[^1] or an early step, add a **Date** field (e.g. "Meeting date", "Event date", "Contract expiration date"). It has to be a real **Date** field: Tallyfy won't save a deadline action pointed at a text or number field. If the field is blank when the automation runs, the deadline action does nothing and the step keeps its default deadline. So make the field required, or add an "is not empty" condition like the examples below.

2. **Add preparation steps**

   Add steps to your [template](/products/pro/documenting/templates/) for the work that needs to happen before that date (e.g. "Send meeting agenda", "Finalize event venue").

3. **Create deadline automations**

   Set up automations that [calculate deadlines](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/actions/deadline-actions/) backwards from your date field:

   **Example - meeting reminder 1 day before:**
   ```automation
   IF field "Meeting Date" is not empty
   THEN shift deadline of "Send meeting reminder" by 1 day before field "Meeting Date"
   ```

   **Example - event prep 2 weeks before:**
   ```automation
   IF field "Event Date" is not empty
   THEN shift deadline of "Finalize catering order" by 14 days before field "Event Date"
   ```
</Steps>

## Real-world examples

### Sales funnel

**Kick-off form field**: "Scheduled meeting date"

```automation
IF field "Scheduled Meeting Date" is not empty
THEN shift deadline of "Complete prospect research" by 3 days before field "Scheduled Meeting Date"
```

```automation
IF field "Scheduled Meeting Date" is not empty
THEN shift deadline of "Send meeting agenda" by 1 day before field "Scheduled Meeting Date"
```

```automation
IF field "Scheduled Meeting Date" is not empty
THEN shift deadline of "Meeting day checklist" by 2 hours before field "Scheduled Meeting Date"
```

Valid deadline units are minutes, hours, days, weeks, and months.

### Podcast publishing

**Kick-off form field**: "Podcast launch date"

```automation
IF field "Launch Date" is not empty
THEN shift deadline of "Upload final audio" by 1 week before field "Launch Date"
```

```automation
IF field "Launch Date" is not empty
THEN shift deadline of "Create social media posts" by 3 days before field "Launch Date"
```

```automation
IF field "Launch Date" is not empty
THEN shift deadline of "Final quality check" by 1 day before field "Launch Date"
```

### International shipping

**Step 1 fields**: Location (dropdown) and Expected arrival date (date field)

```automation
IF field "Location" is "International"
THEN show "Verify customs documentation"
```

```automation
IF field "Location" is "International"
AND field "Expected Arrival Date" is not empty
THEN shift deadline of "Verify customs documentation" by 1 week before field "Expected Arrival Date"
```

This automation's first condition checks **Location**, so the second one checks the arrival date is filled in. If **Expected arrival date** is blank when step 1 is completed, the automation doesn't run and the customs step keeps its default deadline. Once someone fills the date in and completes step 1 again, the deadline counts back a week from it.

International shipments won't get stuck at customs, as long as the arrival date is filled in.

## Advanced patterns

### Multiple deadlines from one date

Create separate automations that all reference the same date field:

```automation
IF field "Contract End Date" is not empty
THEN shift deadline of "Initial renewal discussion" by 60 days before field "Contract End Date"
```
```automation
IF field "Contract End Date" is not empty
THEN shift deadline of "Prepare renewal proposal" by 45 days before field "Contract End Date"
```
```automation
IF field "Contract End Date" is not empty
THEN shift deadline of "Send renewal offer" by 30 days before field "Contract End Date"
```

Each one is a separate automation - they'll all fire independently based on the same date.

### Conditional countdown deadlines

Different lead times based on other field values:

```automation
IF field "Event Type" is "Small Meeting"
AND field "Event Date" is not empty
THEN shift deadline of "Room setup" by 1 hour before field "Event Date"

IF field "Event Type" is "Large Conference"
AND field "Event Date" is not empty
THEN shift deadline of "Room setup" by 1 day before field "Event Date"
```

### Working hours are not applied here

A countdown deadline is set by an automation, and a deadline set by an automation isn't adjusted
to your organization's [work week](/products/pro/settings/org-settings/how-to-adjust-task-deadlines-to-work-week/)[^2].
Tallyfy counts back in calendar time and leaves the answer where it lands, so "14 days before the
event" can land on a Sunday.

Allow for that when you choose the gap. If a task really can't be due at the weekend, pick a gap
that clears it, or set the deadline on the step itself, where the work week is applied.

## Tips

- **Add buffer** - use 2 days before instead of 1 for critical tasks
- **Name tasks clearly** - include timing like "Send agenda (1 day before meeting)"
- **Guard against blank dates** - a deadline action does nothing while its date field is empty, so make the field required if the countdown matters
- **Set fallback deadlines** - add default deadlines on steps in case date fields are left empty
- **Test around weekends** - verify deadlines calculate correctly near non-working days

:::note[When a countdown deadline gets set, and when it moves]
Tallyfy sets a countdown deadline at one of two moments, whichever applies to your automation:

1. When the process launches, if one of its conditions reads a kick-off form field.
2. When somebody completes a step whose form fields its conditions read, such as the step that carries the date field.

If the date changes later, the deadline follows it. For a kick-off date, re-open the kick-off
form, change the date and click **Complete**. For a date on a step, reopen that step, change the
date and complete it again. Tallyfy works the countdown out again from the new date.

Saving a new date without completing the form or step moves nothing. A deadline someone changed
by hand on the task keeps that date. See [when an automation
runs](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/when-automations-run/).
:::
