A countdown deadline makes a [task](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/tasks/) due a set time before a date you already know. If a meeting is on the 14th, the agenda can be due on the 13th, whenever the process happened to start. Meetings, events, launches, contract renewals and compliance filings all work this way.

:::caution[Dates that change after launch]
Each countdown automation runs **once** in a process. If the target date changes after that, the
deadlines it set stay where they are and you change them by hand. A countdown suits a date that's
fixed once it's known, like a contract end date.
:::

## Set up a countdown deadline

<Steps>
1. **Add a date field for the target date**

   In your kick-off form[^1] or an early step, add a **Date** [form field](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/tasks/what-are-form-fields-in-tallyfy/), such as "Meeting date" or "Contract end date". It has to be a Date field, because Tallyfy won't save a deadline action that points at a text or number field. Make it required if the countdown matters.

2. **Add the preparation steps**

   Add a step to your [template](/products/pro/documenting/templates/) for each piece of work that has to happen before that date, such as "Send the agenda".

3. **Add a deadline automation**

   The automation points a [deadline action](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/actions/deadline-actions/) at one step and counts back from the date field:

   ```automation
   IF field "Meeting date" is not empty
   THEN shift deadline of "Send the agenda" by 1 day before field "Meeting date"
   ```
</Steps>

You can count back in minutes, hours, days, weeks or months. The deadline action counts back with **Min (-)**, and it can count forward from the date with **Plus (+)**.

Each preparation step gets its own deadline action, and all of them read the same date field. None of them depends on another. You can build them as separate automations, or add several deadline actions to one automation.

## Dates with and without a time

A date field with no time counts from 5:00 pm on that day, in your organization's timezone. So "1 day before" a meeting on the 14th lands at 5:00 pm on the 13th.

For a countdown in hours or minutes, turn on **Collect time** on the date field so people enter the time as well. Without it, "2 hours before" a meeting on the 14th means 3:00 pm on the 14th, whatever time the meeting starts.

## When the deadline is set

Tallyfy checks a countdown automation at these moments:

- When the process launches, if one of its conditions reads a [kick-off form](/products/pro/launching/triggers/kick-off-forms/) field. If the kick-off form is completed after launch, it's checked then.
- When someone completes or reopens a step that one of its conditions reads.

Once the conditions match, the automation runs and won't run again in that process. Completing the step again with a new date doesn't move the deadline, so [set the task's date by hand](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/tasks/edit-task/change-a-task-due-date/) instead. [When an automation runs](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/when-automations-run/) covers this in more detail.

If the date field is empty when the conditions match, the deadline action does nothing and the task keeps its default deadline. The automation still counts as run, so filling in the date later changes nothing. That's why every example here checks that the date **is not empty**: the automation waits until there's a date to count back from. Give these steps a sensible default deadline as well, in case the date never arrives.

## A different lead time for each case

When the lead time depends on another answer, add a second condition and build one automation per case:

```automation
AUTOMATION Room setup for a small meeting
IF field "Event type" is "Small meeting"
AND field "Event date" is not empty
THEN shift deadline of "Room setup" by 1 day before field "Event date"

AUTOMATION Room setup for a conference
IF field "Event type" is "Large conference"
AND field "Event date" is not empty
THEN shift deadline of "Room setup" by 2 weeks before field "Event date"
```

## Show a step and set its deadline

A step can appear only for some processes and still count down to a date. In this example, step 1 asks for the shipment's location and expected arrival date:

```automation
AUTOMATION Show the customs check
IF field "Location" is "International"
THEN show "Verify customs documentation"

AUTOMATION Customs check deadline
IF field "Location" is "International"
AND field "Expected arrival date" is not empty
THEN shift deadline of "Verify customs documentation"
     by 1 week before field "Expected arrival date"
```

These are two automations because their conditions differ. The customs step should appear as soon as the location is known, while its deadline also needs the arrival date. A single automation would show the step and then be used up before anyone entered the date.

## Working days and hours

A deadline set by an automation isn't adjusted to your organization's [work week](/products/pro/settings/org-settings/how-to-adjust-task-deadlines-to-work-week/)[^2]. Tallyfy counts back in calendar time and leaves the result where it lands, so "14 days before" an event can fall on a Sunday. Pick a gap that clears the weekend, or set the deadline on the step itself, where the work week is applied.
