## What's conditional logic?

Conditional logic is the "IF" half of a Tallyfy [automation](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/) - it lets your [processes](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/processes/) make decisions automatically. When a condition is true, the automation runs its "THEN" action, such as showing a hidden step, changing who's assigned or setting a deadline. Automations are set on the [template](/products/pro/documenting/templates/), and every process launched from it follows them.

:::tip[How a condition reads]
Tallyfy doesn't use flowcharts for this. Each condition reads as a short statement, such as "IF Approval Step Is rejected". Administrators, and Standard members given edit permission on the template, can change it. Light members can't edit templates.
:::

### Available condition types
You can base conditions on:
- [Form field](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/tasks/what-are-form-fields-in-tallyfy/) answers - including kick-off form fields and step form fields (e.g. "if budget exceeds $10,000")
- Step status - completed, reopened, approved, rejected, acknowledged, or expired
- Assignment status - whether a step has no one assigned (`not_assigned`)
