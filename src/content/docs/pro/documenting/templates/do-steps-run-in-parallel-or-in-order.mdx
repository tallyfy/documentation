## Steps run in parallel by default

When you launch a process, every step is open at once. Anyone assigned to a step can start and finish it straight away, in any order. Nobody waits for a coworker unless you tell Tallyfy they should.

Most work doesn't need a strict order, and making people wait for an unrelated step only slows the process down.

## Make a step wait for another step

Add an automation to the template. To hold step 7 until step 3 is done:

**IF** `Step 3` **is** `Completed` **THEN** **Show** `Step 7`

A step that a **Show** rule targets is hidden when the process launches, so you don't hide it separately. It appears as soon as step 3 is completed.

## Run steps in a strict order

Chain the same rule down the template. Step 1 completed shows step 2, step 2 completed shows step 3, and so on. People then see one step at a time.

## Wait for two steps at once

To hold a step until two earlier steps are both done, put both conditions in one rule and join them with **AND**. The [synchronization example](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/examples/synchronization/) shows how.

## Mix the two

Most processes need both. Let steps that don't depend on each other run side by side, and add a rule only where one step has to wait. In project-planning terms, every step starts at launch, and each rule adds a finish-to-start link.

Every visibility rule is covered in [automate hiding or showing tasks](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/actions/visibility-actions/).
