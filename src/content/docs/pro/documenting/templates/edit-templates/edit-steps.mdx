## Editing steps in templates

In a Tallyfy [template](/products/pro/documenting/templates/), you can add steps and change any step's description, assignees, type, form fields, deadline and advanced settings.

:::note[Who can edit steps?]
[Members](/products/pro/documenting/members/) with a "Light" role can't edit templates or steps. Only "Standard" members (if allowed by an Admin) or "Administrator" members can.
:::

### Views in the template editor

In the new Tallyfy client, the template editor has one view switcher at the top. A procedure template offers five views of its steps:

*   **Table** lists the steps, and it's where you edit them.
*   **Blocks** shows each step as a tile, with one band per [milestone](/products/pro/documenting/templates/milestones/). A tile shows when the step is due, such as "Day 2" or "+3 d after step 9". A dashed edge with an hourglass means a rule decides whether the step appears, and a lightning mark means another rule may change it. Click a tile to open the step.
*   **Timeline** shows every step's deadline on one timeline. It used to be called **Timings**. See [the Timeline view](/products/pro/documenting/templates/edit-templates/step-timings/#the-timeline-view-in-the-new-client).
*   **Map** draws the template's [automations](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/), with the steps no rule touches listed below.
*   **Document** shows the template as one read-only page.

A form template offers **Table** and **Document**. A document template offers **Edit** and **Document**. **Automations** has its own button beside the switcher, and the switcher stays there while you work on rules, so one click takes you back to your steps. The new client isn't generally available yet.

### Adding new steps

Open your template in Edit mode, scroll to the bottom, click **Add Step**, type a name, and press Enter.

### Modifying existing steps

To modify a step:
- Go to the **Templates** library
- Find your template and click the **Edit** icon (pencil symbol)

![Template edit mode with pencil icon highlighted](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy%2Fpro%2Fdesktop-light-open-edit-mode-template.png)

In edit mode, click any step to open its details panel on the right. Here's what you can change:

#### Step description
Write instructions for the [task](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/tasks/). You can add text, formatting, images, files, links, tables, videos, [variables](/products/pro/documenting/templates/variables/), and [snippets](/products/pro/documenting/templates/snippets/).

<Steps>
1. Click the step you want to edit.
2. Click the **Description** area in the details panel.
   ![Editing a step description in the details panel](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy%2Fpro%2Fdesktop-light-edit-step-description.png)
3. Use the toolbar to format text, add links, images, etc. See [Insert Media](/products/pro/documenting/templates/insert-media/) for details.
4. Tallyfy saves changes automatically.
</Steps>

:::tip[Use videos for clear instructions]
For complex tasks, embed a short screen recording in the description. Pick **Video** from the **Insert** menu, then upload a file or paste a YouTube or Vimeo link.
:::

#### Assignment

<Steps>
1. Click the step you want to assign.
2. Go to the **Assign** tab in the details panel.
3. Pick an assignment method:
    - **Specific Person/Group**: Pick members or groups from the list.
    - **[Job Title](/products/pro/documenting/templates/edit-templates/understanding-assignment-types/)**: Assign by role (e.g., "Account Manager").
    - **[Guest](/products/pro/documenting/guests/how-can-i-assign-tasks-to-guest-users-in-tallyfy/)**: Assign to someone outside your company via email.
    - **Nobody right now**: Leave unassigned initially (see note below).
</Steps>

*   **"Nobody right now"**: What happens at runtime depends on the **Assign launcher automatically** setting (in the Advanced tab). It's ON by default - the person starting the process gets assigned. If OFF, the task stays unassigned.
*   **Assigning Groups**: [Create the group first](/products/pro/documenting/groups/how-to-manage-groups-and-assign-tasks-in-tallyfy/) in Tallyfy before assigning a task to it.

![Assigning a task to a member in the step details panel](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy%2Fpro%2Fdesktop-light-assign-a-task-to-somebody.png)

#### Step type
The type sets the buttons on the task. A **Task** step shows **COMPLETE**, an **Approve/Reject** step shows **APPROVE** and **REJECT**, and an **Expiring** step shows **ACKNOWLEDGE**. The two email types send an email. See [Step Types Explained](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/tasks/what-types-of-tasks-can-i-create-with-tallyfy/) for details.

<Steps>
1. Click the step you want to change.
2. Click the **Type** dropdown in the details panel and pick the new type.
</Steps>

:::tip[Prevent task debt with expiring tasks]
Use the "Expiring" type for FYI items or optional info. These tasks auto-complete at deadline if not done manually, so non-critical items don't pile up as [task debt](/products/pro/tutorials/how-to/how-to-avoid-task-debt/). Weekly updates and informational announcements are typical uses.
:::

:::tip[Email branding options]
Email step types (Email Draft and Email Auto-Send) include a "Send Chromeless" option that removes Tallyfy branding from sent emails. Use this when emails should look like standard messages. See [Step Types](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/tasks/what-types-of-tasks-can-i-create-with-tallyfy/) for details.
:::

#### Form fields

<Steps>
1. Click the step.
2. Go to the **Form fields** tab in the details panel.
3. Click **Add field** to create a new one, or click an existing field to edit it.
</Steps>

See [Types of Form Fields](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/tasks/what-are-form-fields-in-tallyfy/) for details.

#### Deadlines and timings

<Steps>
1. Click the step.
2. On the **Basics** tab, click the **Timings** row to expand it.
3. Set the **deadline** (when work should be complete) relative to:
    - **Process Launch**: Due X time **after** the process starts (default).
    - **Another Step**: Due X time **after** another specific step. That counts from the other step's due date while it's still open, then from its actual completion time once it's done.
    - **Future Step Completion**: Due X time **before** a *later* step's due date. Useful for working backward from a final deadline (e.g., "Draft due 3 weeks before Publish Date").

   The gap has to be at least 1, and it can't reach further than 1000 weeks. Full detail on how a deadline is worked out, including where your work week applies, is in [how a step deadline is worked out](/products/pro/documenting/templates/edit-templates/step-timings/).
   ![Setting a deadline relative to another step completion](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy%2Fpro%2Fdesktop-light-setup-deadline-after-completion-example.png)
   ![Setting a deadline before a future step](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy%2Fpro%2Fdesktop-light-deadline-before-future-step.png)
4. Choose **Start On-Time** or **Start Anytime**:
    - **Start On-Time**: Suggests when work should begin. Guidance only - not enforced.
    - **Start Anytime**: No suggested start time (default).
</Steps>

:::note[Start time vs deadline]
- **Start time**: When work should **begin**. Helps with planning but isn't enforced.
- **Deadline**: When work must be **complete**. This is the due date that determines overdue status.
- Neither one makes a step wait for another step. Every step is open from launch unless an automation holds it back. See [do steps run in parallel or in order?](/products/pro/documenting/templates/do-steps-run-in-parallel-or-in-order/)

Both can be set for process tasks (via the template editor) and one-off tasks. Example: start time of "Monday 9 AM" and deadline of "Wednesday 5 PM".
:::

:::note[Overall process deadlines]
Your process's overall deadline depends on which task has the latest due date from launch. A deadline based on another step's completion, like "3 days after Step 2 finishes," shifts whenever that step's completion date shifts.
:::

:::tip[Changing a lot of deadlines at once]
Click **Tools** in the template editor toolbar and use the
**Deadlines** tab to change deadlines across many steps in one go. Anyone who can edit the
template can use it. See [template tools](/products/pro/documenting/templates/how-can-power-tools-enhance-my-tallyfy-experience/).

The Deadlines tab only sets deadlines that count from process launch, not from another step. Two other approaches:

1. **Template duplication** - build a master template with your standard deadlines, then duplicate it and adjust
2. **Professional services** - Tallyfy's team can help with large-scale template setup, at support@tallyfy.com
:::

#### Advanced settings

- **Launch another process when complete**: "Yes" auto-starts another process (from a different template) when this task finishes. You can inject tasks into the current process or start a separate one. See [Launch another process guide](/products/pro/launching/launch-process-when-task-is-completed/).
- **All assignees must complete this task**: "Yes" means the task isn't done until *every* assignee marks it complete. An Administrator who isn't assigned can still click Complete, but each click completes it on behalf of one assignee who hasn't finished, so the task stays open until everyone's part is done.
- **Assign launcher automatically**: ON by default. If a step has *no* assignees, the person who launches the process gets assigned. Turn OFF to leave it unassigned.
- **Task is mandatory and cannot be removed at launch**: "Yes" means this step can't be removed when someone launches the process.
- **Only assigned members can complete or edit**: "Yes" restricts the task to assigned people only (Administrators always have access).
- **Emit a webhook when this task is completed?**: "Yes" sends the whole process as JSON to the URL you enter, via a [webhook](/products/pro/integrations/webhooks/), when this task is completed.
- **Prevent guests from making or seeing comments**: "Yes" hides comments from any [guests](/products/pro/documenting/guests/) assigned to this task.
- **Unique Step Alias**: An internal ID for this step, used for [webhook](/products/pro/integrations/webhooks/) and [API](/products/pro/integrations/open-api/) integrations.
- **Tags**: Add tags to this step for filtering and organization.

:::note[Webhooks and integrations]
For more on [Webhooks](/products/pro/integrations/webhooks/) and connecting Tallyfy to other tools, see the [Integrations](/products/pro/integrations/) section.
:::
