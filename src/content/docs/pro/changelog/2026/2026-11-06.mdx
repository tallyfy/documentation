### Changes

- **[API]** Daily [digests](/products/pro/settings/personal-settings/how-can-i-manage-email-notifications-in-tallyfy/#which-tasks-the-digest-lists) now list overdue tasks and tasks due within 30 days. Tasks due later come into the digest by themselves once they're that close.
- **[UI]** Administrators can change the digest window under [Settings > Organization > Customization](/products/pro/settings/org-settings/organization-customization-settings/), from 3 to 90 days or no limit.

### Removed

- **[API]** Removed the unused option to hide tasks with a future start date from digests. No organization had turned it on.

### Bug fixes

- **[API]** Guest digests now keep the one-off tasks due soonest, so guests with many tasks see the ones due next.
