## Managing email notifications

To change your settings:
1. Click your profile picture/name (top right) > **Settings**.
2. Go to **My Account** > **Email Notifications**.

Each switch reads **Yes** or **No**. Under **NOTIFICATION EMAILS** you'll find these, in this order:

- **Receive emails when I'm assigned**: emails that tell you a task is yours.
- **Email me about minor changes to a task where I'm assigned**: a task is reopened, its summary is edited, it's hidden or shown, it's added to a folder, it gets a tag, or its type changes.
- **Email me about deadline changes on tasks where I am assigned**: someone changes the deadline on one of your tasks. A deadline that moves because an earlier step was completed doesn't send this email.
- **Email me about comments on tasks where I am assigned**
- **Email me when I am @mentioned on a task**
- **Email me when a task is completed**: someone else completes a task you're assigned to.
- **Email me about assignment changes on tasks where I'm assigned**
- **Email me when a process is completed**

**Receive emails when I'm assigned** covers the email you get when a process launches with a step of yours visible, the "It's your turn" email when a rule shows you a hidden step, and the email when someone assigns you to a task that's already running. It also covers reminders someone sends you by hand, and your copy of the email an email step sends. Turn it off and all of those stop. It doesn't turn off the other switches on this page or your digest, so there's no single switch that stops every email.

When you post a comment, Tallyfy doesn't email you about it. [Who gets an email about a comment](/products/pro/integrations/email/emails-tallyfy-sends/comments-mentions-and-digests/#who-gets-an-email-about-a-comment) explains who does get one.

![Tallyfy email notification settings screen](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy%2Fpro%2Fdesktop-light-change-email-notification-settings.png)

:::note[When you launch a process yourself]
Launching a process doesn't stop your own emails. If one of your steps is visible from the start, you get the launch email like every other assignee, as long as **Receive emails when I'm assigned** is on.
:::

### Digest emails (reminder settings)

Tallyfy sends digest emails at 6 AM in your local timezone. Your digest lists your overdue tasks first, then ones coming up.

*   **Choose your days**: Pick which days of the week you want to receive the digest, under **Set which days your email digest will be sent**. The default is Monday, Wednesday, and Friday.
*   **Turn it on or off**: Use **Email me a daily task list**, under **DIGEST EMAILS**.
*   **Troubleshooting**: If someone isn't getting reminders, check that their daily digest is switched on and that today is one of their chosen days.

### Deadlines and reminders

Tallyfy doesn't send a reminder before a deadline, or when a deadline passes. A deadline is the date a task should be done by. It doesn't hold back the email that tells someone a task is theirs. An [Email Auto-Send step](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/tasks/what-types-of-tasks-can-i-create-with-tallyfy/#what-happens-at-the-deadline) is different, because the email it carries goes out at its deadline.

Once a deadline passes, the task shows as overdue, and the digest lists it on your digest days. You can also [send a reminder by hand](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/tasks/how-can-i-send-email-reminders-in-tallyfy/) for a specific task. It reaches each member who has **Receive emails when I'm assigned** switched on, and the guests on the task. On an Email Draft or Email Auto-Send step, it doesn't reach guests.

### Which tasks the digest lists

Your digest lists your overdue tasks and the tasks due within your organization's digest window. The window is 30 days unless an Administrator changes it. A task due later stays in Tallyfy as normal, and it comes into the digest by itself once it's within the window. Tasks with no deadline never appear in the digest, so give a task a deadline if you want it there.

When a window is set, a line at the bottom of each digest says how many days it covers.

To change the window, an Administrator goes to **Settings > Organization > Customization** and picks **No limit**, 3, 7, 14, 30, 60 or 90 days. The choice covers every member's and guest's digest in the organization. **No limit** removes the cutoff, so a task can show however far off its deadline is. See [organization customization settings](/products/pro/settings/org-settings/organization-customization-settings/) for the other digest options.
