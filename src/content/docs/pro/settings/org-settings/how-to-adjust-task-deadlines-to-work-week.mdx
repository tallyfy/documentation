## Adjusting task deadlines to match your work week

Tallyfy can shift most [deadlines](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/tasks/) so they don't land on non-working days or outside business hours. An Administrator configures this once, and it applies organization-wide.

### Configuring working days and hours

<Steps>
1. Go to **Settings** > **Organization** > **Customization**.
2. Under **Default work week**, select your work week length - 7-day, 6-day (Mon-Sat), 5-day (Mon-Fri), or 4-day (Mon-Thu).
3. Under **Default Working Hours**, toggle each day between **All hours** (any time counts) or **Fixed hours** (set a start and end time with the slider).

   ![Tallyfy work week and working hours configuration settings](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy%2Fpro%2Fdesktop-light-change-workweek.png)

4. Click **Save Changes**. Tallyfy uses these settings from then on, in the places listed below.
</Steps>

:::note[How deadlines adjust]
If a deadline falls on a non-working day or outside your set working hours, Tallyfy **always
pushes it forward** to the next working day. It never moves a deadline backward, not even for a
deadline expressed as "before" something. A Saturday deadline moves to Monday morning, assuming
Monday is a workday.

On a day set to **All hours** it lands at midday, because there's no start time to use. On a day
with **Fixed hours** it lands at the start of those hours.

Set no working days at all and nothing is adjusted. Deadlines then land wherever the arithmetic
puts them.
:::

### Where the work week applies, and where it doesn't

This is worth reading, because a deadline that ignores your work week usually looks like a bug
rather than a boundary.

| How the deadline got its date | Work week applied |
|---|---|
| Worked out when the task was created, at launch or later | Yes |
| Changed by hand on a running task | Yes |
| Moved because the step it counts from was completed or edited | Yes |
| Set by an [automation's deadline action](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/actions/deadline-actions/) | **No** |

An automation that says "3 days before the event date" does exactly that arithmetic and leaves
the answer alone, so it can land on a Sunday in a Monday to Friday organization. Allow for it
when you choose the gap.

Setting an exact date by hand is adjusted, which surprises people in the other direction. Pick
6pm on a day whose working hours end at 5pm and the deadline moves to the next working morning
rather than staying at 6pm.

### What's not supported yet

Tallyfy's deadline calculations use the work week pattern you configure - but they don't account for:

- **Country-specific holidays** (e.g., Labor Day, Boxing Day)
- **Organization-specific holidays** (e.g., company closure days, annual shutdown)
- **Person-specific unavailability** (e.g., vacation days, sick leave)

:::tip[Public holiday data coming soon]
Tallyfy maintains a dataset of national holidays for 200+ countries at [tallyfy.com/national-holidays](https://tallyfy.com/national-holidays/). A future release will integrate this data into deadline calculations, so deadlines automatically skip national holidays in your country.
:::
