## Troubleshooting member deletion

Can't delete a member from your Tallyfy organization? You must disable a member before you can permanently delete them. Here's how to fix common blockers.

### Quick diagnosis

1. **Check member status** - Active, Disabled, or Invited?
2. **Check their role** - Are they an Administrator?
3. **Count admins** - How many active admins in the organization?
4. **Note the exact error** - What message appears?

### Common error messages

#### "Please disable the user before deletion."

Tallyfy requires a two-step process - disable first, then delete:
1. Go to **Settings** > **Organization** > **Members**
2. Click the member's name
3. Click **Deactivate account**, followed by their name
4. If the **Automatic conflict resolution** page opens, hand their work to one person, or skip the handover
5. Then permanently delete them

#### "Cannot modify the default administrator."

The full error reads: *"Cannot modify the default administrator. Please assign another member as default administrator first."*

It appears when the member is an Administrator and no other active Administrator exists. To fix it, promote another member to Administrator, then deactivate the original member. If the member you're removing is the default administrator, Tallyfy passes that role to another active Administrator for you.

You'll also see it on the **Automatic conflict resolution** page if the member is the default administrator and you hand their work to someone who isn't an Administrator. That person would become the default administrator, so pick an Administrator.

#### "Bot users cannot be disabled."

Bot accounts (like Tallyfy Bot) can't be removed or disabled. They don't count toward billing - they handle automated comments and system tasks.

When removing a member through the `DELETE /organizations/{org}/users/{user_id}` endpoint, you'll see *"You can't remove a bot user from this organization!"* instead.

#### No other admin available

If you try to disable the only administrator, you'll get the "Cannot modify the default administrator" error. Promote any Standard member to Administrator first, then remove the original admin.

### Special scenarios

#### Member shows as removed but still has access

1. Clear browser cache and cookies
2. Verify status shows "Deactivated"
3. Check for active API tokens - revoke them manually
4. If it's still happening after 30 minutes, contact support

#### Can't find a deactivated member

1. Go to the **Members** list
2. Use the **Status** column's filter and choose **Deactivated**
3. Search by name or email

#### Bulk member removal

For multiple deletions via API:
- Always disable before deleting (two-step process for each)
- Space out requests to avoid rate limits
- Process in reasonable batches

### System behavior details

#### Email and name changes

When you permanently delete someone who doesn't belong to any other Tallyfy organization, Tallyfy:
- Appends "(Deleted)" directly to the last name (no space) - e.g. `Smith` becomes `Smith(Deleted)`
- Changes email to `{email}.deleted.{user_id}` - e.g. `john@company.com` becomes `john@company.com.deleted.12345`

This prevents conflicts if you re-invite that person later. If they belong to another organization, their name and email don't change.

#### What happens to member data

**Removed immediately**:
- Login access
- Active sessions

If the person also belongs to another Tallyfy organization, permanent deletion only removes them from yours. Their account and their other organizations aren't touched. If they don't belong anywhere else, their whole Tallyfy account is deleted.

**Kept for compliance**:
- Task completion history (name shows with "(Deleted)")
- Process participation records
- Comments and attachments
- Audit trail entries

#### Handing over their work

One person takes over everything. On the **Automatic conflict resolution** page you pick one active member. Tallyfy moves the templates the member created, owns or belongs to, and their template step assignments. It also moves the processes they started, own or belong to, the tasks they created, and every task assigned to them. Their [scheduled jobs](/products/pro/launching/triggers/via-recurring-schedule/) move too, if the new person can run them. The new person gets an email. The member is taken out of all their groups, and any access they had to specific templates or processes stays with them.

Work never falls to "Tallyfy Bot". The bot can't be assigned tasks at all (see [member statuses](/products/pro/settings/org-settings/understanding-member-statuses/)). When a deactivation skips the handover, on the page or through the API without `with_reassignment=true`, the work stays with the deactivated member. Nothing moves on its own, so reassign it yourself when you're ready. Their scheduled jobs fail until you change each job's owner, so change the owners before you deactivate them.

### API deletion

**Two-step sequence**:
```
1. DELETE /organizations/{org}/users/{user_id}/disable
2. Wait for 200 response
3. DELETE /organizations/{org}/users/{user_id}/delete
```

**Deactivate with reassignment**:
```
DELETE /organizations/{org}/users/{user_id}?with_reassignment=true&to={new_user_id}
```

This call hands the member's templates, processes, tasks and scheduled jobs to the specified member and then deactivates the member. `to` must be an active member of your organization, other than the member you're removing. It doesn't delete them. To delete them permanently, call `DELETE /organizations/{org}/users/{user_id}/delete` afterwards.

### Edge cases

#### Organization with a single member
- You can't delete the last member
- Contact support for organization closure

### Recovery

Accidentally deleted someone? Contact support immediately with:
- Organization name
- Member email
- Deletion date

Recent deletions may be restorable. For older ones, send a new invitation.

### When to contact support

- Error messages don't match those listed here
- Server errors (500-series) appear during deletion
- Deletion seems stuck for over an hour

**Provide**: organization name, member email, exact error message, screenshot

### Prevention tips

- Keep at least 2 administrators at all times
- Document who the default administrator is
- Use consistent offboarding procedures
