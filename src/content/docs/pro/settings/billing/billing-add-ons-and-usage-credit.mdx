## Two kinds of add-ons

Tallyfy has two kinds of add-ons that sit on top of your seats. **Analytics** is a yearly add-on priced per seat, charged on every full and light seat. **Usage credit** (AI credit and per-task credit) is a pre-paid balance you buy upfront and draw down as you use it. AI also comes with a free AI tier every month, so you only need AI credit, or your own Anthropic key, for AI use beyond that. You manage all of this from **Settings** > **Billing**.

## Analytics add-on

Analytics costs $20 per seat, per year, charged on every committed seat (full and light). Turn it on with a single toggle. Your annual amount is your total seats times $20. On invoiced accounts the larger charge appears at your next renewal rather than straight away. It scales automatically: add seats mid-term and your Analytics charge grows to match, with no separate step.

For multi-entity accounts, Analytics appears as a single line across all your linked workspaces on your invoice.

## Free AI tier

Every organization with AI turned on gets a free AI tier, an allowance of AI use for each billing month. The allowance grows with the number of seats on your plan, and everyone in your organization shares it. On a trial or on the free plan, it grows with your active members instead, counting up to 5.

AI is on unless Tallyfy support turned it off for your organization. While it's off, you get no free tier. [Contact Tallyfy Support](/products/pro/miscellaneous/support/how-can-i-contact-tallyfys-support-team/) to turn it back on.

To see how much you've used, go to **Settings** > **Billing** > **Overview** and find the **AI usage and credit** card. It shows your use as a percentage, such as "Free AI tier: 40% used this month", along with the date it resets. Each billing month starts with a fresh free tier, and anything unused doesn't carry over.

## When the free tier runs out

Tallyfy uses your free AI tier first each month, then your AI credit. If you've added your own Anthropic key, all AI runs on that key instead, and neither the free tier nor your credit is used.

With no credit left and no key of your own, AI stops until the next billing month, or until someone adds credit or a key. Tallyfy AI shows a notice titled **AI is paused** in the conversation, or on the task the AI was working on, with a message and buttons:

*   **Administrators** see "Your free AI tier for this month is used up. Add AI credit or your own Anthropic key, or contact support." The buttons are **Buy AI credit**, **Add your own key** and **Contact support**.
*   **Everyone else** sees "Your free AI tier for this month is used up. Ask your admin to add AI credit or your own Anthropic key, or contact support." The buttons are **Ask your admin**, which opens your member list so you can see who your administrators are, and **Contact support**.

If your organization has had AI credit before and the balance is now empty, the message also says your AI credit has run out.

## AI credit

AI credit is a pre-paid balance in US dollars. On a card account, you buy it with the card on your organization's billing account. On an invoiced account, the purchase goes on a Xero invoice instead, and the credit is added to your balance once that invoice is paid.

Once the month's free tier is used up, each AI request draws down your balance, which never goes below zero. The **AI credit balance** on the **AI usage and credit** card shows what's left.

### Buy AI credit

<Steps>
1. Go to **Settings** > **Billing** > **Overview**.
2. On the **AI usage and credit** card, under **Buy AI credit**, pick $25, $50, $100 or $250.
3. Click **Buy credit**.
4. Check the amount, then click **Buy** with the amount, such as **Buy $25**. On a card account, Tallyfy charges the card on file and adds the credit to your balance. On an invoiced account, the credit is added once the Xero invoice is paid.
</Steps>

### Auto top-up

Auto top-up is on by default. Your first purchase sets the amount each top-up buys. When your balance falls below 20% of that amount, Tallyfy charges the card on file for the same amount again. For example, after you buy $25, a $25 top-up runs when your balance falls below $5. Buying more credit later doesn't change the top-up amount.

To stop automatic charges, switch off **Auto top-up** on the same card. Your balance then goes up only when you buy credit, and AI stops once both the balance and the month's free tier are used up.

If a top-up is declined, or still fails after Tallyfy retries it, auto top-up turns off. Tallyfy emails your billing contacts about it: your administrators, the account owner, the billing owner and any extra billing addresses on the account. The card shows a warning with an **Update card** button. Update your card, then switch auto top-up back on.

Auto top-up isn't available for invoiced accounts.

## Your own Anthropic key

An administrator can add your organization's own Anthropic key in **Settings** > **AI** > **Your own key**. All AI then runs on that key, and Anthropic bills your organization directly. Tallyfy doesn't charge you for that AI use. See [use your own Anthropic key](/products/pro/settings/billing/use-your-own-anthropic-key/).

## AI credit from Tallyfy support

Tallyfy support can add AI credit to your organization without charging your card. Support records a reason with each grant. Granted credit shows in your **AI credit balance**, and AI uses it the same way as credit you bought.

## The AI usage log

The AI usage log lists every AI request. **Paid by** shows **Free tier**, **Free tier and AI credit**, **AI credit** or **Your key**. **Charged** shows a dollar amount only for requests paid entirely from AI credit. See the [AI usage tab of the system log](/products/pro/settings/org-settings/system-log/#ai-usage-tab).

## Per-task credit

Per-task credit is a pre-paid balance too. You buy an amount upfront, and Tallyfy draws it down as tasks complete. Your balance updates once a day.

When your per-task credit runs out, your work is never blocked. Tasks keep completing normally and Tallyfy keeps recording the usage, so Tallyfy records what it could not cover as a shortfall against your balance, which stops at zero rather than going negative. You'll be asked to settle it.

:::note[Per-task credit pricing]
Pricing for per-task credit is being finalized. [Contact Tallyfy Support](/products/pro/miscellaneous/support/how-can-i-contact-tallyfys-support-team/) for current rates and to add per-task credit.
:::

## How you pay for add-ons

*   **Card (automatic) accounts** pay for add-ons and credit top-ups instantly by card.
*   **Invoiced and multi-entity accounts** get add-ons and credit on their Xero invoice, paid by ACH or wire transfer. [Contact Tallyfy Support](/products/pro/miscellaneous/support/how-can-i-contact-tallyfys-support-team/) to arrange these.
