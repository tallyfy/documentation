:::note[MCP industry standard]
BYO AI uses MCP (Model Context Protocol), the industry standard for connecting AI to business tools. In December 2025, MCP was donated to the Agentic AI Foundation under the Linux Foundation - co-founded by Anthropic, Block, and OpenAI, with AWS, Google, Microsoft, Cloudflare, and Bloomberg as supporting members. All major AI platforms now support MCP.
:::

## Put your AI subscription to work inside Tallyfy workflows

BYO AI (Bring Your Own AI) connects the AI subscriptions you already have directly into your Tallyfy workflows. That includes a paid Claude plan (Pro, Max, Team, or Enterprise), a ChatGPT Business, Enterprise, or Edu workspace (ChatGPT Pro connects with read access only), Google Gemini, and Microsoft 365 Copilot. When you ask it in a chat, your AI can complete tasks, write documents, analyze data, and recommend decisions in your running processes.

BYO AI lets that AI work on your actual workflow steps, using the subscription you're already paying for.

**Your AI updates Tallyfy from email and chat.** Connect the AI you already use. It fills in fields and completes tasks.

### What the AI can do in a task

Without BYO AI, using AI in a workflow means copying text from a document, pasting it into ChatGPT, waiting for a response, then copying that back into another system.

With BYO AI, you ask your AI about a task in a chat, and it can:
- Read the task context and form field data
- Follow your instructions
- Complete the task or add detailed comments

BYO AI doesn't start on its own when a task arrives. Your AI checks Tallyfy only when you ask. To have AI react to an event, such as a completed task, send a [webhook](/products/pro/integrations/webhooks/) to middleware or your own code that calls the AI.

### How authentication works

BYO AI authenticates through OAuth2 - the same secure method you use when signing into websites with Google or Microsoft.

1. **You authorize once** - connect your AI account to Tallyfy through a secure OAuth flow
2. **Your AI platform stores the token** - it keeps a secure sign-in token, never your password
3. **AI acts on your behalf** - when you ask in a chat, your AI calls Tallyfy as you, using your subscription
4. **Results show in the chat** - Tallyfy makes the change, such as updating or completing a task, and your AI reports back

## Why Tallyfy is the AI control layer

| What Tallyfy does | Why it matters in the age of AI |
|---|---|
| Define process steps | You can't automate anything without a recipe. |
| Hand small tasks to AI | Massive reduction in mistakes, omissions, and hallucination. |
| People approve work | Accountability. You can't blame AI for mistakes. |
| Hybrid people + AI tasks | AI can't do every task in a process. |
| Track real-time status | Tracking AI sessions at scale is a nightmare. |
| Gradually shift tasks to AI | A total re-do will break a process that works today. |

### Key capabilities

#### Task completion
Your AI can complete tasks when you ask, based on:
- Form field values from previous steps
- Document attachments and their contents
- Historical process data and patterns
- Custom instructions embedded in templates

#### Content generation
Your AI can draft content from what's in the process:
- Draft emails based on process context
- Reports from collected data
- Summaries of long documents
- Translations into multiple languages

#### Decision support
AI analyzes data and provides recommendations:
- Risk assessment based on form responses
- Approval recommendations with reasoning
- Priority scoring for incoming requests
- Anomaly detection in submitted data

#### Process optimization (planned)
This isn't available yet. It's planned as Phase 3 in the [implementation status](#implementation-status) below. The plan is for AI to learn from your workflow patterns and:
- Suggest process improvements
- Identify bottlenecks
- Recommend task reassignments
- Predict completion times

### Common use cases

**Document processing** - AI reads a contract you share in the chat, extracts key terms, and fills in the form fields. No more manual data entry from PDFs.

**Customer service** - AI drafts responses to customer inquiries based on your knowledge base and previous successful responses. Agents review and send.

**Compliance reviews** - AI checks submissions against your compliance rules, flags issues, and suggests corrections before human review.

**Meeting follow-ups** - AI reads meeting transcripts, extracts action items, and creates follow-up tasks in the right processes.

**Data analysis** - AI analyzes spreadsheet uploads, generates insights, and creates summary reports without anyone opening Excel.

### Implementation status

BYO AI is live and actively developed:

**Phase 1 - Direct integration** - available now
- OAuth 2.1 connection to all major AI platforms (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot)
- Task completion and commenting via natural language
- MCP server with full tool support

**Phase 2 - Advanced automation** - in development
- Multi-step AI workflows
- Conditional AI actions based on responses
- AI-to-AI handoffs between steps

**Phase 3 - Learning and optimization** - planned
- AI learns from your process history
- Suggests template improvements
- Predictive automation

### Security and control

- **Subscription isolation** - each organization uses their own AI subscription
- **Data boundaries** - AI only accesses data from the specific process context
- **Audit trails** - every AI action is logged with inputs and outputs
- **Human oversight** - configure which tasks require human review
- **Revocation** - disconnect AI access instantly if needed
