## Everything Tallyfy sends about a running process

A process is a template that somebody launched and is now running. These messages track it as a whole rather than a single task, so most of them carry a progress bar.

Not the one you're after? The [full gallery](/products/pro/integrations/email/emails-tallyfy-sends/) covers all 53.

### A guest was invited to work on tasks

Guests get this when somebody invites them into a process and assigns them specific tasks.

No picture here yet. Look for **Guest Invitation** in small type near the top of the message.

### Who is assigned to a task changed

Tallyfy sends this when the people assigned to a task change, listing who was added and who was taken off.

No picture here yet. Look for **Owner Changed** in small type near the top of the message.

### You were added to a running process

Being added as a collaborator on a live process triggers this, with the tasks that are now yours.

![Tallyfy collaborator added email naming the process, with progress at three of five and a task and due date table](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy/pro/email-runs-collaborators-run-step-owners.png)

### You now own a process

When somebody makes you the owner of a running process, Tallyfy tells you and shows what's outstanding.

![Tallyfy notice that a person was made owner of a named process, with an outstanding task table and Review process button](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy/pro/email-runs-notices-process-owner-changed.png)

### A process was updated

Tallyfy sends this when somebody changes a process you're part of.

![Short Tallyfy notice that a named person updated a running process, with a View process button](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy/pro/email-runs-notices-run-update.png)

### A process finished

Once every task in a process is done, Tallyfy closes the loop with this and a full progress bar.

![Tallyfy notice that a process was completed, showing a full progress bar at five of five tasks](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy/pro/email-runs-run-completed.png)

### A process you watch was launched

If you watch a template, Tallyfy tells you each time somebody launches a new process from it.

![Tallyfy notice that a named process has been launched, with a View process button](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy/pro/email-runs-run-launched-member-watched.png)

### Activity on a process you're watching

Watching a process means Tallyfy emails you when something happens on it, and says what happened.

![Tallyfy watch alert addressed by name about activity on a watched process, noting assignees were updated](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy/pro/email-runs-run-watching.png)

### A task was completed

Tallyfy sends this when somebody finishes a task in a process you follow, with the process progress underneath.

![Tallyfy notice that a person completed a task, with a task and due date table and process progress at three of five](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy/pro/email-runs-task-completed.png)

Every message on this page carries a **Manage email preferences** link in its footer, so you can [change how often Tallyfy emails you](/products/pro/settings/personal-settings/how-can-i-manage-email-notifications-in-tallyfy/) whenever you like.
