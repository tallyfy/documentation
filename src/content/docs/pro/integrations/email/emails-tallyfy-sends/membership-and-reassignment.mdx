## When somebody's work moves to you

When somebody leaves a team or hands their work over, the work has to land somewhere. These messages tell the new owner what they've picked up.

Not the one you're after? The [full gallery](/products/pro/integrations/email/emails-tallyfy-sends/) covers all 53.

### Your account is active again

A colleague turning a deactivated member back on triggers this, with the date and time it happened.

No picture here yet. Look for **Account Reactivated** in small type near the top of the message.

### Work was reassigned to you

When somebody hands their open work over to you, Tallyfy names them as the previous owner so you know where it came from.

![Tallyfy notice that tasks were reassigned, naming the previous owner and offering a View your tasks button](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy/pro/email-accounts-users-reassign.png)

### Work was reassigned to you, in English

It's the same handover notice, sent in English when that's the language your account is set to.

No picture here yet. Look for **Tasks Reassigned** in small type near the top of the message.

Every message on this page carries a **Manage email preferences** link in its footer, so you can [change how often Tallyfy emails you](/products/pro/settings/personal-settings/how-can-i-manage-email-notifications-in-tallyfy/) whenever you like.
