## Everything Tallyfy sends about a single task

This is the biggest group, and the one people ask about most. Anything that happens to a task you own can reach you here: a new assignment, a nudge, a moved deadline, an edit.

Not the one you're after? The [full gallery](/products/pro/integrations/email/emails-tallyfy-sends/) covers all 53.

### You were given a one-off task

Somebody assigning you a task that isn't part of a process sends this, with the task name and its due date.

![Tallyfy task assignment email with a task and due date table and a green Complete task button](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy/pro/email-standalone-tasks-task-assigned.png)

### An email task

Some steps in a process are built to reach you by email rather than in the app, and this is what one looks like.

No picture here yet. Look for **Email Task** in small type near the top of the message.

### A task was sent to you again

Anyone can re-send you a task you haven't finished, and this reminder is what lands in your inbox.

![Tallyfy task reminder naming the process and the person who re-sent it, with the task and due date](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy/pro/email-tasks-resend-task.png)

### It's your turn

When a colleague finishes their step and a rule hands the next one to you, Tallyfy tells you it's your turn and shows how far the process has got.

![Tallyfy your turn email showing process progress at three of five and a table of two tasks with due dates](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy/pro/email-tasks-revealed-by-rule.png)

### A plain task email with no branding

A few task emails are sent as plain text with no logo, header or footer, which is why one can look unbranded next to the rest.

![A plain Tallyfy task email showing only the task instruction text on a white background, with no header or footer](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy/pro/email-tasks-send-chromeless.png)

### A task deadline moved

If the due date on one of your tasks changes, Tallyfy shows the old date crossed out beside the new one.

![Tallyfy deadline change email showing the old due date struck through above the new one, with process progress](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy/pro/email-tasks-task-deadline-changed.png)

### A task was added to your process

Tallyfy sends this when a new task appears in a process you're already part of.

No picture here yet. Look for **Process Linked** in small type near the top of the message.

### A task was reopened

Completing a task isn't always the end. If somebody reopens one, this tells you it's back on your list.

No picture here yet. Look for **Task Reopened** in small type near the top of the message.

### A task was edited

When somebody edits a task assigned to you, Tallyfy shows the wording before and after so you can see what actually changed.

![Tallyfy task update email showing the task summary before and after an edit, with process progress](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy/pro/email-tasks-task-update.png)

### Activity on a task you're watching

Watching a task means Tallyfy emails you when it changes, and this is that alert.

No picture here yet. Look for **Watch Alert** in small type near the top of the message.

### Activity on a template you're watching

Watching a template means you hear about edits to it, including which step somebody changed.

![Tallyfy watch alert naming the template and the step that changed, with View template and Manage alerts links](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy/pro/email-tasks-watching-blueprint.png)

Every message on this page carries a **Manage email preferences** link in its footer, so you can [change how often Tallyfy emails you](/products/pro/settings/personal-settings/how-can-i-manage-email-notifications-in-tallyfy/) whenever you like.
