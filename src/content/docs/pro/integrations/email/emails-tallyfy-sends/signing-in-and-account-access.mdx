## Getting into Tallyfy, and what those emails look like

These arrive when you're getting into Tallyfy, or when something about your access changes. Two of them carry a link that expires, so check the timestamp before you worry that a link is broken.

Not the one you're after? The [full gallery](/products/pro/integrations/email/emails-tallyfy-sends/) covers all 53.

### Account reactivated automatically

Tallyfy sends this when a suspended account is switched back on by itself. You can sign in again straight away, and you don't need a new password.

No picture here yet. Look for **Account Reactivated** in small type near the top of the message.

### Account reactivated by your company

You get this when someone in your company turns your suspended account back on. Your old password still works.

No picture here yet. Look for **Account Reactivated** in small type near the top of the message.

### Welcome to Tallyfy

This arrives the moment your account goes live, with a sign-in link and a short guide to building your first template.

No picture here yet. Look for **Account Activated** in small type near the top of the message.

### Reset your password

You get this when you ask to reset your password. The link inside works for one hour, and ignoring the email leaves your password alone.

![Tallyfy password reset email with a reset button and a note that the link expires in one hour](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy/pro/email-accounts-auth-recover-password.png)

### Password reset finished

Tallyfy sends this once your reset is complete, so you know you're able to sign in with the new password.

![Tallyfy confirmation that a password reset finished, addressed by name with a log in button](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy/pro/email-accounts-auth-reset-confirmation.png)

### Verify your email address

This goes out when you sign up. Confirm your address and the account's ready to use.

![Tallyfy email verification message with a green Verify email button and an Action Required badge](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy/pro/email-accounts-auth-verify-email.png)

Every message on this page carries a **Manage email preferences** link in its footer, so you can [change how often Tallyfy emails you](/products/pro/settings/personal-settings/how-can-i-manage-email-notifications-in-tallyfy/) whenever you like.
