## Comments, mentions, and the once-a-day summary

If you'd rather not hear about every single comment, the digest gathers the day into one message instead.

Not the one you're after? The [full gallery](/products/pro/integrations/email/emails-tallyfy-sends/) covers all 53.

### Who gets an email about a comment

Tallyfy emails a new comment to the people assigned to that task, and to anyone who's @mentioned in it. Each person gets it only if the matching switch in their [email settings](/products/pro/settings/personal-settings/how-can-i-manage-email-notifications-in-tallyfy/) is on. People watching the task hear about it too, either straight away or in a digest.

You're never emailed about a comment you wrote yourself, even one where you @mention someone else. Launching or owning a process doesn't add you either. If you want a process's comments, get assigned to its tasks, or ask people to @mention you.

When someone marks a comment as an improvement, Tallyfy also posts a copy on the matching step of the template and @mentions the template's owner, so the owner hears about it if their @mentions switch is on.

To check this works, ask a second person to comment on a task you're assigned to and @mention you. If you test on your own, nothing arrives, because every comment is yours.

### Somebody mentioned you in a comment

Typing your name into a comment pulls you in, and Tallyfy sends you the comment itself so you don't have to go looking.

No picture here yet. Look for **@Mentioned** in small type near the top of the message.

### Your daily digest

Rather than emailing you about every task, Tallyfy can send one summary a day grouped by process, with overdue items called out in red.

No picture here yet. Look for **Daily Digest** in small type near the top of the message.

### A template was shared with you

Tallyfy sends this when somebody gives you access to one of their templates.

![Tallyfy notice that a named person shared a template, with a View template button](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy/pro/email-messaging-guests-checklist-permissions.png)

### Your daily task summary, for guests

Guests get one summary a day instead of a message per task, grouped by process with overdue work flagged in red.

![Tallyfy daily digest for guests listing tasks grouped by process, each with a progress bar and due dates, overdue items in red](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy/pro/email-messaging-guests-digest.png)

### An issue was reported

Anyone can flag a problem on a task, and this brings you the task, the process and what they wrote.

![Tallyfy issue reported email naming the task and reporter, quoting the report, with a Respond to issue button](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy/pro/email-messaging-problem-report.png)

### An issue was resolved

When a reported problem is cleared, Tallyfy closes the loop with the original text still attached.

![Tallyfy issue resolved email naming who resolved it and the task, quoting the original report, with a View issue button](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy/pro/email-messaging-problem-resolve.png)

### A new comment on a step

Tallyfy sends the comment text itself, with the process and step it belongs to, so you can judge it without opening anything.

![Tallyfy new comment email naming the commenter and step, with the comment quoted and a View comment button](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy/pro/email-messaging-step-new-comment.png)

### A new comment on your task

Somebody commenting on a task assigned to you sends this, with the comment text included.

No picture here yet. Look for **New Comment** in small type near the top of the message.

Every message on this page carries a **Manage email preferences** link in its footer, so you can [change how often Tallyfy emails you](/products/pro/settings/personal-settings/how-can-i-manage-email-notifications-in-tallyfy/) whenever you like.
