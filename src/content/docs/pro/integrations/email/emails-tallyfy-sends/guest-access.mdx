## What a guest receives instead of a password

Guests don't have a password. They get a private link instead, and that link is what the email below carries.

Not the one you're after? The [full gallery](/products/pro/integrations/email/emails-tallyfy-sends/) covers all 53.

### Your guest access link

Guests sign in with a private link instead of a password, so this arrives whenever a guest asks for that link again.

No picture here yet. Look for **Guest Access** in small type near the top of the message.

Every message on this page carries a **Manage email preferences** link in its footer, so you can [change how often Tallyfy emails you](/products/pro/settings/personal-settings/how-can-i-manage-email-notifications-in-tallyfy/) whenever you like.
