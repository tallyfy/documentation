## Confirming your membership, with or without company sign-on

Both of these confirm the same thing, that your membership is live. Which one you get depends on whether your company signs in with its own login system.

Not the one you're after? The [full gallery](/products/pro/integrations/email/emails-tallyfy-sends/) covers all 53.

### Your membership is confirmed

Tallyfy sends this once your membership is set up, pointing you at your profile page to fill in your details.

![Tallyfy membership confirmation naming the organization, with a link to the profile page and a Sign in button](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy/pro/email-accounts-users-password-login.png)

### Your membership is confirmed, with single sign-on

This is the same confirmation for companies that sign in through single sign-on, so the main button uses your company login.

![Tallyfy membership confirmation offering an Accept via SSO button and a manual alternative link](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy/pro/email-accounts-users-password-login-via-sso.png)

Every message on this page carries a **Manage email preferences** link in its footer, so you can [change how often Tallyfy emails you](/products/pro/settings/personal-settings/how-can-i-manage-email-notifications-in-tallyfy/) whenever you like.
