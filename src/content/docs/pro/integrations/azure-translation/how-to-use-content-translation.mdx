## Use content translation in Tallyfy

*(Note: This feature requires Administrator setup of the [Azure Cognitive AI integration](/products/pro/integrations/azure-translation/how-to-set-up-azure-cognitive-ai-integration/) first.)*

Tallyfy's content translation automatically translates user-generated text in real-time using Microsoft Azure AI. Each team member reads content in their preferred language, across 100+ supported languages.

It's separate from the [UI language setting](/products/pro/integrations/azure-translation/), which controls buttons and menus. Content translation handles text your team writes into templates and tasks, such as task titles, task descriptions and form field labels.

### What gets translated

Content translation covers visible text that you and your team create:

- Task titles and task descriptions.
- Form field labels and their guidance text.
- The text of a template or process when you read it as a document.

### What stays in the original language

- Comments added to tasks or processes.
- Answers people type into form fields.
- Tallyfy's interface elements. Use the separate [UI language setting](/products/pro/integrations/azure-translation/) for those.
- Standard system messages.

## How to set up your content language

Everyone sees a globe icon in Tallyfy's top menu bar. Once your organization has the Azure AI connection configured, its **Content Language** section lets you pick a language. The globe animates when translation is actively processing.

<Steps>
1. Click the globe icon in the top menu bar.
   ![Tallyfy content translation menu in the top bar](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy%2Fpro%2Fdesktop-light-content-translation-menu.png)
2. In the **Content Language** section, click **Edit** and choose your preferred language from the dropdown.
3. Click **Save**. Tallyfy translates visible text on screen as you browse.
</Steps>

### Where translations appear

- **In templates**: The template's text displays in your chosen language when you view it as a document.
- **In processes**: The process text displays in your chosen language when you view it as a document.
- **In tasks**: Task titles, task descriptions, form field labels and field guidance show up translated. Comments and the answers typed into form fields stay as written. A task title or description you write is saved in your language but appears translated to others based on *their* preference.

### Tips for better translations

- Write original text clearly and keep sentences short.
- Avoid slang, jargon, or culturally specific phrases.
- Have a native speaker review important translated content when possible.

### Switching languages

<Steps>
1. Click the globe icon in the top menu bar.
2. Choose a different language and save.
3. Content refreshes with translations in the new language. Tallyfy remembers your choice.
</Steps>

To turn off translation, select **No Translation** from the content language dropdown.

### How it works for multilingual teams

- Each person picks their preferred reading language independently.
- Everyone sees the same information, just translated for them.
- Task titles and descriptions written in one language appear translated for users with a different language preference. Comments and form answers stay in the language they were typed in.
- Numbers and dates stay consistent across all languages.
