## Improve task management with Tallyfy and Zapier

Tallyfy handles the human side of [process](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/processes/) management - judgment, creativity, and decisions. Zapier handles the robotic parts and connects Tallyfy to your other apps.

Your team completes [tasks](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/tasks/) that need human input while Zapier triggers and actions handle the repetitive work. The Tallyfy connector supports launching [processes](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/processes/), creating standalone tasks, completing tasks, changing assignees and deadlines, and adding comments - all from Zapier.

:::note[Consider other middleware too]
Zapier works for simple connections, but [middleware](/products/pro/integrations/middleware/) tools like **n8n**, **Make**, or **Microsoft Power Automate** often handle more advanced [integrations](/products/pro/integrations/) at lower cost.

If you have developers, Tallyfy's [Open API](/products/pro/integrations/open-api/) gives you full control.
:::

### Why use Tallyfy instead of automation-only tools?

Tallyfy covers the steps that need a person to review, decide, or approve something:

- **Human-centric workflows** - [Processes](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/processes/) are made of [tasks](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/tasks/) that people complete, including [approval tasks](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/tasks/what-types-of-tasks-can-i-create-with-tallyfy/) with **APPROVE** and **REJECT** buttons.
- **Structured task management** - The steps in your template become tasks with assignees and deadlines when a process launches, so it's clear who owns each one and when it's due.
- **Cross-app [integration](/products/pro/integrations/)** - When someone completes a task, Tallyfy's **Task completed** trigger can start a Zap that updates your other apps.

### Key integration use cases

Each pattern below pairs a trigger from another app with a Tallyfy action in Zapier.

#### 1. CRM-triggered onboarding

When a deal closes in your CRM, Zapier fires a trigger and Tallyfy's "Launch Process" action starts your client onboarding [process](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/processes/). Its tasks go to the [members](/products/pro/documenting/members/) set as assignees in the template or in the Zap, and anyone can check progress in the [tracker view](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/tracker-view/) instead of emailing for a status update.

#### 2. Scheduled process launches

Zapier's schedule trigger pairs with Tallyfy's "Launch Process" action to [launch](/products/pro/launching/) your [processes](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/processes/) on a recurring schedule, such as a weekly report or a monthly audit. [Automate monthly process launch with Zapier](/products/pro/integrations/middleware/zapier/how-to-automate-monthly-process-launch-with-zapier/) shows the steps.

#### 3. Communication-based task creation

Requests that arrive by email or Slack can become tracked work in Tallyfy. An email to support@yourcompany.com can become a standalone [task](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/tasks/) with an assignee and a deadline. A Slack keyword can kick off an entire [process](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/processes/).

#### 4. Web form tracking

A customer fills out a web form, and Tallyfy's "Launch Process" action [launches](/products/pro/launching/) a [process](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/processes/) with the answers mapped into its kick-off form fields. To launch a different process for each request type, add a filter or paths step in Zapier before the Tallyfy action. Each request is then tracked from submission until its last task is done.
