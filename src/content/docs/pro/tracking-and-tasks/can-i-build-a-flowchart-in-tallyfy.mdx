## How Tallyfy compares to flowcharts, spreadsheets, and docs

Tallyfy isn't for building flowcharts - it runs and tracks your processes instead of just drawing them. You can still see the shape of a process, though. The new Tallyfy client draws it for you in two views, **Blocks** and **Map**.

### See the shape of a process with Blocks and Map

The new Tallyfy client isn't generally available yet. In it, the view switcher at the top of a process, or of a template in the editor, offers two views that show a process as a picture. You don't draw either one. Tallyfy builds them from the template's steps, [milestones](/products/pro/documenting/templates/milestones/) and [automations](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/).

*   **Blocks** shows every step as a tile, with one band per milestone. Done tiles fade, late ones have red due text, and a dashed edge means a rule decides whether that step appears. Hover over a tile, or select it, to outline the steps it depends on and tint the steps it affects.
*   **Map** draws your rules. Steps that a rule reads or changes sit in a diagram, joined by lines. Every other step sits below the diagram in Blocks bands.

Blocks has no lines between steps because Tallyfy doesn't make steps wait for each other. Every step is open from launch unless an automation holds it back, so a line from one step to the next would show an order that isn't there. See [do steps run in parallel or in order?](/products/pro/documenting/templates/do-steps-run-in-parallel-or-in-order/) and [ways to look at a process](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/processes/#ways-to-look-at-a-process).

### Why Tallyfy beats flowcharts

Flowcharts show a process but can't *run* it.

*   **Instructions, videos, and files on each step**: Attach what people need right where they need it. No hunting for that PDF guide.
*   **Real-time status**: See exactly where each process stands. Is John stuck on step 3? You'll know instantly.
*   **Deadlines and automatic reminders**: Assign tasks to specific people with due dates - Tallyfy nudges them automatically.
*   **People actually use it**: Your team works through each process in Tallyfy as the work happens, so it stays current.

### Why Tallyfy beats spreadsheets

Tracking processes in spreadsheets gets messy fast - multiple versions, nobody updating status, broken formulas.

Tallyfy fixes that:

*   **One place for everything**: All your processes live in Tallyfy, so nobody has to check several spreadsheets.
*   **Clear ownership**: Each task shows exactly who's responsible. No more "I thought Sarah was handling that."
*   **Order where it matters**: Steps run in parallel by default, and automations hold a step back or route tasks based on earlier answers.
*   **Permissions built in**: Contractors see only their tasks. Managers see everything.
*   **Always current**: Forget "Process_Tracker_v2_FINAL_FINAL.xlsx" - everyone works from the same live workflow.

### Why Tallyfy beats documents

Word docs and PDFs store information but can't run processes.

*   **Jump to your step**: No scrolling through pages - go straight to where you are.
*   **Works on any device**: Use Tallyfy on a phone, a tablet or a laptop.
*   **Track who's doing what**: See who's completed steps and where things are stuck.
*   **Active, not static**: Tallyfy guides people through each step, sends reminders, and tracks completion automatically.
