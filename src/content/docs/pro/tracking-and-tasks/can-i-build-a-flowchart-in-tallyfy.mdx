## How Tallyfy compares to flowcharts, spreadsheets, and docs

Tallyfy isn't for building flowcharts - it runs and tracks your processes instead of just drawing them. You can still see the shape of a process, though. The new Tallyfy client draws it for you in two views, **Blocks** and **Map**.

### See the shape of a process with Blocks and Map

The new Tallyfy client isn't generally available yet. In it, the view switcher at the top of a process, or of a template in the editor, offers two views that show a process as a picture. You don't draw either one. Tallyfy builds them from the template's steps, [milestones](/products/pro/documenting/templates/milestones/) and [automations](/products/pro/documenting/templates/automations/).

*   **Blocks** shows every step as a tile, with one band per milestone. Done tiles fade and show a check, late ones have red due text, and a dashed edge means a rule decides whether that step appears. Hover over a tile, or select it, to outline the steps it depends on and tint the steps it affects.
*   **Map** draws your rules. Each rule sits in a diagram with the steps it reads or changes, joined by lines. Every other step sits below the diagram in Blocks bands.

This template has 21 steps and three rules. The rules touch three of the steps, so the map draws those and lists the other 18 below.

![Map view of a template, with three rules and the steps they touch in a diagram and the other steps in bands below](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy/pro/desktop-light-template-editor-map-view.png)

Blocks has no lines between steps because Tallyfy doesn't make steps wait for each other. Every step is open from launch unless an automation holds it back, so a line from one step to the next would show an order that isn't there. See [do steps run in parallel or in order?](/products/pro/documenting/templates/do-steps-run-in-parallel-or-in-order/) and [ways to look at a process](/products/pro/tracking-and-tasks/processes/#ways-to-look-at-a-process).

### Why Tallyfy beats flowcharts

In Tallyfy, each step carries the instructions, videos and files people need, so nobody goes looking for a PDF guide. Each task goes to a named person with a due date, and Tallyfy sends them reminders. You can see where every process stands, down to which step is late and who has it.

<lite-vimeo videoid="338954164"></lite-vimeo>

### Why Tallyfy beats spreadsheets

A tracking spreadsheet ends up in several versions, with statuses nobody updates and formulas that break. In Tallyfy, every process lives in one place and everyone works from the same live copy. Each task shows who's responsible for it.

Steps run in parallel by default. Where order matters, automations hold a step back or route tasks based on earlier answers. Permissions are built in too: contractors see only their own tasks, and managers see everything.

![Creating a client onboarding template with Tallyfy AI](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy%2Fpro%2Fdesktop-light-create-client-onboarding-template-from-tallyfyAI.png)

![Clicking create template by Tallyfy AI button](https://screenshots.tallyfy.com/tallyfy%2Fpro%2Fdesktop-light-click-create-template-by-tallyfyai.png)

### Why Tallyfy beats documents

A Word doc or a PDF can't tell anyone what to do next. Tallyfy takes each person straight to their step, on a phone, a tablet or a laptop, and sends them reminders. It records who completed each step, so you can see where things are stuck.
